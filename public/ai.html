BETA

It's new, and it says so

What to expect

  • It can be wrong. Check a price before you buy.
  • Answers and pictures will keep getting better week to week.
  • Some things take a few seconds while it looks them up.
  • Your feedback shapes it. Tell us in the support server.

What it won't do

  • Moderate your server, change settings or run giveaways. It points you to the right command.
  • See anyone's private lists, or yours without your say.
  • Ask for a password, a key or a payment.
  • Remember anything you didn't ask it to. Say "forget everything" any time.

Quick questions

Is it free?

Yes, during the beta. Servers can bring their own AI key, and a daily cap keeps usage in check.

Does it work without an AI key?

Partly. Without one, the bot still answers the most common questions itself: prices, free games, your wishlist, library stats, what to play, and how-to guides.

Who can see my data?

It answers from your own data, for you. Group features like "play together" only use friends who opted in to sharing in that server. Export or delete everything any time from /me mydata.

Why did it reply with a picture?

Because a graph or a table reads faster than a paragraph. The text under it stays short on purpose.

It got something wrong.

That's what the beta is for. Use /bot bug or the support server and say what you asked.
