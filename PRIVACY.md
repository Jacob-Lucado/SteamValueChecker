# Privacy Policy: Steam Value Checker

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Steam Value Checker is a Discord bot that prices a list of games. This page explains what it does with your data.

## What the bot receives

- The game titles you type, or the titles in a file you upload.
- The region, store, sort and `paid` options you choose.
- Your Discord user ID, only to enforce a short cooldown between requests and to let you page through your own results. It is held in memory and not saved.

## What the bot does not want

- **Game keys.** Anything that looks like a product key is discarded before any lookup. Please do not post keys.
- **Passwords, cookies or logins** for Steam, Fanatical or any other service. The bot never asks for them.

## What it does with the data

- Game titles are sent to **IsThereAnyDeal** (isthereanydeal.com) to look up prices. Their [privacy policy](https://isthereanydeal.com/privacy/) applies to that request. Nothing about you, other than the titles, is sent.
- Results are shown only to you (ephemeral replies) and are returned as an embed and a CSV file in that reply.

## Storage

- **No database.** Nothing about you or your lists is stored.
- Prices are cached in memory for about 30 minutes and cleared when the bot restarts.
- User input and results are never written to logs. Error logs contain only technical error messages.
- Discord keeps your messages and the bot's replies according to Discord's own policies.

## Sharing

The bot does not sell, share or transfer your data, other than the titles sent to IsThereAnyDeal as described above.

## Your control

Because nothing is stored, there is nothing to delete. You can remove the bot from your server at any time, and you can delete the reply in Discord.
