## Services and data

- **Steam** (Valve): Game, price and profile data come from the Steam Web API and store. [store.steampowered.com/](https://store.steampowered.com/)
- **IsThereAnyDeal** (IsThereAnyDeal): Cheapest-price and deal data. [isthereanydeal.com/](https://isthereanydeal.com/)
- **Fortnite-API** (Fortnite-API.com): Item shop, news, map and cosmetics on the Fortnite page. [fortnite-api.com/](https://fortnite-api.com/)
- **Discord** (Discord Inc.): Sign-in, servers and the bot itself. [discord.com/](https://discord.com/)
- **emoji.gg** (emoji.gg): Emoji packs. Each pack has its own license on emoji.gg. [emoji.gg/](https://emoji.gg/)
- **YouTube, SoundCloud, Spotify** (their owners): Players are embedded straight from each service. [www.youtube.com/](https://www.youtube.com/)
- **GamerPower** (GamerPower): Free game and giveaway listings. [www.gamerpower.com/](https://www.gamerpower.com/)
- **SomaFM, Radio Paradise, NTS** (the stations): Radio streams in the music player. [somafm.com/](https://somafm.com/)

## Programs it runs

- **FFmpeg** (LGPL / GPL): Runs as a separate program to decode audio for music. [ffmpeg.org/](https://ffmpeg.org/)
- **yt-dlp** (Unlicense): Runs as a separate program to find playable streams. [github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp)
- **Chromium** (BSD-3-Clause and others): Opens pages that have no API, for a few seconds at a time. [www.chromium.org/](https://www.chromium.org/)
